What it does

Drives track themselves Start the app once and forget it. GPS runs only while you're actually driving, then powers back down.
Swipe to classify Right for business, left for personal. MileHQ learns your regular destinations and suggests the rest.
IRS-ready exports CSV and PDF with every required field, per-category totals, audit flags, and a SHA-256 integrity digest.
72.5¢ per business mile 2026 IRS standard rates built in, and fully editable if your situation differs.
CarPlay and Siri Start and end drives from the car screen, or just say "Start a drive in MileHQ."
Nothing held hostage Unlimited trips and unlimited exports. Your records are always yours to take with you.

Privacy is the product

MileHQ has no account, no servers, and no analytics. Your trips and routes are recorded and stored only on your iPhone, which means we cannot read them, sell them, or lose them. A mileage tracker knows where you sleep, who your clients are, and when you visit the doctor — that belongs to you alone.
